Privacy Policy · 1.1.0

Rack Sort does not require a developer account. Your board, progress, preferences, undo history and tool or reward records are stored on your device so you can resume play and receive each reward once. If you use Game Center, Apple handles your game player ID and the number of levels completed for the leaderboard. Your Apple settings control profile visibility. Game Center does not sync your game save.

For rewarded and interstitial ads we use Google AdMob and its User Messaging Platform (UMP). Unity Ads and Liftoff Monetize (Vungle) are included as mediation partners and may process ad data when used. These services can process IP addresses and approximate location, device or app identifiers, consent choices, ad views and interactions, and performance or crash information for ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, privacy choices and diagnostics. Ad services may also process app-open events and information about the device and its connection.

The app requests non-personalized ads and does not ask for Apple tracking permission. Technical data may still be used for ads. Where available, you can change or withdraw your choices in Settings under Ad Privacy Options. Local data stays on your device until removed, including when you delete the app. Game Center data and ad-provider data have separate controls and retention under their respective policies.

Guiquan Chen (cgq-source) maintains Rack Sort and this support site. If you contact support, we use your address, message and any attachments you choose to send to handle the request and keep them as needed for that purpose or applicable obligations. Email us to request access, correction or deletion. GitHub Pages hosts this site and may process connection data, such as IP addresses. We add no site analytics or tracking scripts.

Privacybeleid · 1.1.0

Je hoeft geen account bij de ontwikkelaar te maken. Het speelbord, je voortgang, voorkeuren, geschiedenis van ongedaan gemaakte zetten en gegevens over hulpmiddelen en beloningen staan op je apparaat. Je Apple-instellingen bepalen de zichtbaarheid van je profiel. Game Center synchroniseert je spelvoortgang niet.

Voor advertenties met beloning en schermvullende advertenties gebruiken we Google AdMob en het User Messaging Platform (UMP) van Google. Unity Ads en Liftoff Monetize (Vungle) zijn geïntegreerde bemiddelingspartners en kunnen bij gebruik advertentiegegevens verwerken. Deze diensten kunnen IP-adressen en geschatte locaties, apparaat- of app-ID’s, toestemmingskeuzes, advertentieweergaven en interacties, en prestatie- of crashgegevens verwerken voor advertentieweergave, meting, fraudepreventie, keuze-instellingen en diagnose. Advertentiediensten kunnen ook app-openingen en informatie over het apparaat en de verbinding verwerken.

De app vraagt niet-gepersonaliseerde advertenties aan en vraagt niet om trackingtoestemming van Apple. Voor advertenties kunnen nog steeds technische gegevens worden gebruikt. Als de instellingen privacyopties voor advertenties tonen, kun je je keuzes daar aanpassen of intrekken.
